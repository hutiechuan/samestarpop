Privacy Policy

Same Star Pop · Last updated: October 3, 2026

Same Star Pop (“the App”) is a free puzzle game supported by advertising and optional in-app purchases. This policy explains what information is handled, by whom, and why.

What the App Itself Stores

Your game progress (level, score, board, coins, items and the “Remove Ads” status) is stored only on your device. The App has no accounts, no login, and runs no servers of its own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as:

DataPurpose
Device advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking in the iOS promptPersonalized ads and attribution
IP address and approximate (coarse) locationAd delivery, fraud prevention, regional compliance
Device information (model, OS version, language, screen size)Ad delivery and performance
Ad interaction data (views, clicks, watch time of rewarded videos)Measurement, limiting ad frequency, paying rewards
Crash and performance diagnosticsKeeping the SDK stable

If you do not allow tracking, ads are still shown but are not personalized using the advertising identifier. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you will see a consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later from the in-game menu. Google’s own practices are described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In-App Purchases (Apple)

Purchases of coin packs and “Remove Ads” are processed by Apple through the App Store. The App never sees your payment details; it only receives a signed receipt confirming the purchase, which is kept on your device to unlock what you bought. You can restore purchases on any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

Children

The App is rated 4+ but is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents can disable tracking and in-app purchases in iOS Screen Time settings.

Your Choices

  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking: allow or deny tracking for the App at any time.
  • Buy “Remove Ads” to stop interstitial ads. Rewarded videos remain optional and only play when you tap the button.
  • Deleting the App deletes all locally stored progress.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

应用本身保存什么

关卡、分数、棋盘、金币、道具和「去广告」状态只保存在你的设备上。应用没有账号、不登录、没有自己的服务器。

广告（Google AdMob）

应用通过 Google AdMob 展示广告。为投放和统计广告，Google 可能收集：广告标识符 IDFA（仅在你于 iOS 弹窗中允许追踪时）、IP 地址与粗略位置、设备信息、广告互动数据（观看、点击、激励视频观看时长）、崩溃与性能诊断。拒绝追踪后仍会展示广告，但不会用广告标识符做个性化。欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士用户在请求任何广告前会看到同意表单，之后可在游戏菜单中修改。

内购（Apple）

金币包和「去广告」由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，应用不会接触你的支付信息，只收到用于解锁的签名收据。同一 Apple ID 可在任意设备恢复购买。

儿童

应用评级 4+，但不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童个人信息。家长可在 iOS「屏幕使用时间」中关闭追踪与内购。
