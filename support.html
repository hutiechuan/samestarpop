How to play

  1. Tap two or more touching stars of the same colour to select them, tap again to pop.
  2. Score = stars² × 5, so bigger groups pay far more. Pop small groups first so big ones can form.
  3. When no group is left the level ends. The fewer stars left, the bigger the bonus; a clear board gives +2000.
  4. Reach the level target to continue, otherwise it is game over.
  5. Items: 🔨 smash one star · 💣 clear a 3×3 area · 🎨 repaint a star · 🔀 shuffle · 🌈 clear one colour. Earn coins by clearing levels or watching an optional video, and buy items in the shop.

Purchases

If a purchase did not unlock, open the shop and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple ID used to buy. For refunds, use Apple’s Report a Problem page; the App cannot issue refunds itself.

Ads

Interstitial ads appear between levels; buy “Remove Ads” to stop them. Rewarded videos only play when you tap the button and give coins when watched to the end.

怎么玩

  1. 点 2 颗以上相连（上下左右）的同色星星选中，再点一次消除。
  2. 得分 = 颗数² × 5，一次消得越多越划算。先消小组，让同色星星掉到一起再消大组。
  3. 没有可消的组时结算，剩得越少奖励越多，全部消光 +2000。
  4. 分数达到目标进下一关，不够就游戏结束。
  5. 道具：🔨 敲掉 1 颗 · 💣 清 3×3 · 🎨 改颜色 · 🔀 洗牌 · 🌈 清掉一种颜色。过关或观看可选视频获得金币，在商店购买道具。
